ABOUT

Based in the heart of Boston, I am a professional photographer with five years of experience capturing the raw energy, monumental milestones, and unique personalities of New England. My work spans the dynamic energy of live events, the candid stories of Boston's historic streets, and clean, striking headshots that help professionals put their best foot forward. Whether it is documenting the "here, then, and now" of a celebration or freezing a split-second city scene in time, my goal is always to deliver timeless, high-impact imagery. Let’s collaborate and bring your story into focus.
